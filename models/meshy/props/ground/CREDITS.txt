Ground / field textures
=======================

turf_albedo.png       — mown-pitch turf (home, arena, locker, safe zones)
scrapyard_albedo.png  — worn industrial concrete (glitchyard / camp / drill combat zones)
rival_clay_albedo.png — raked ballpark clay (retired from live maps by the Wildlife art pass; kept for reference)
court_albedo.png      — polished court hardwood (finals districts)
wildrange_albedo.png  — herd-trampled range w/ game trails (away_1/2/3 — Wildlife Expanse)
howler_den_albedo.png — packed claw-marked den earth (away_boss, the Howler's Sideline)
basecamp_albedo.png   — cleared campsite ground (basecamp hub)

Source: SELF-GENERATED (no third-party / CC0 asset — no attribution required).
  Generated procedurally by tools/gen_ground_textures.py (pure Python + Pillow).
  256x256, seamless/tileable by construction:
    - low-frequency variation = sum of INTEGER-frequency sinusoids (periodic over the tile)
    - fine grain = a hash of (pixel_x, pixel_y) only (repeats identically when the tile repeats)
  To regenerate or retune the palette:  python3 tools/gen_ground_textures.py

Wired in client/Client.gd via _make_field_material()/_apply_field_theme() (client-only render;
no shared/ change). uv1_scale tiles one repeat every ~5 (turf) / ~6 (court, basecamp) /
~7 (scrapyard, wildrange, howler_den) world-units.
