# Batch 004 prompts

Batch 004 is a reusable arena-structure and field-support set. The props remain isolated and modular; approved furniture from earlier batches can be placed inside or beside them later in Godot.

Proposed preview cost: six Meshy 6 previews at 20 credits each, hard ceiling 120 credits. Refinements are not included and require a separate approval.

## Player tunnel gate

One isolated game-ready futuristic player entrance gate for a premium sports-fantasy MMO arena. Large open walk-through arch with two armored side columns and one connected overhead header, graphite and deep-navy metal, electric-cyan inset rails, small neon-lime ready lights and restrained orange mechanical fasteners. Strong championship broadcast silhouette, approximately 4m wide, 1m deep, 3.5m tall, with a clear rectangular opening and stable flat column feet. Medium-low-poly, finished front and back. No text, logo, doors, player, stairs, wall, room, floor, tunnel, crowd, flags, hologram, transparent parts, floating pieces or surrounding environment. Centered upright as one connected object.
